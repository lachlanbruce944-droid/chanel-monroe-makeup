The results page is dominated by **directories**: Easy Weddings, ABIA, Airtasker, Fresha, Oneflare. Most artists rank through those plus a Google Business Profile, not their own site.

Nobody local owns **SFX / Halloween / character / face paint** searches. That's Chanel's clearest gap to win.

## Done on the site (this round)
- Title: "Mobile Makeup Artist Gold Coast | Bridal, Glam & SFX | Chanel Monroe Makeup"
- Meta description, Open Graph and Twitter tags, en_AU locale, geo meta
- BeautySalon JSON-LD: services, 15 Gold Coast suburbs in areaServed, Instagram in sameAs
- `<noscript>` H1 and intro paragraph, so crawlers that don't run JS still see the keywords. The About page H1 is the brand.
- robots.txt (blocks /tools and /data/raw)
- Favicon set and apple-touch-icon

## To do once the domain is live
1. Add `<link rel="canonical">`, absolute `og:url` / `og:image`, and `url` / `telephone` in the JSON-LD, then add `sitemap.xml` and a `Sitemap:` line in robots.txt.
2. **Google Business Profile** (the biggest single lever): category "Make-up artist", service-area business (hide the address), list the suburbs, add 20+ photos, and ask every client for a review that mentions the service and suburb.
3. Submit the site in Google Search Console and request indexing.
4. Directory listings with the same name and phone everywhere: Easy Weddings, ABIA (if eligible), Oneflare, Airtasker, Fresha, Hello May / Polka Dot Bride vendor guides.
5. Short service pages or sections with their own headings: "Bridal makeup Gold Coast", "Formal & school formal makeup", "Halloween & SFX makeup Gold Coast", "Kids party face painting Gold Coast".
6. Alt text on Chanel's own photos describing the look and the place (e.g. "soft bronzed bridal makeup, Burleigh Heads").
7. Speed: the gallery is image-heavy. Keep images WebP and lazy-load clips.
