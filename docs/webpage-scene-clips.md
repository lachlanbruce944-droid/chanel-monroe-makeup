There are **78 reference entries** including the original 18. The new 60 candidates use 19 videos and target 20 seconds each. They focus on makeup and beauty shoots, weddings, people in sunny coastal settings, beach clubs and drinks, cosplay, and seasonal celebrations.

**No video clips have been downloaded, recorded, uploaded as MP4s or added to the live website.** These are research and selection directions, not a production asset collection. Nineteen new candidates have provisional 20-second windows starting at creator chapter boundaries. The other 41 have locating directions and no exact timestamps. All require visual review; people, uninterrupted movement, captions, usable duration and actual quality must be confirmed. Advertised 4K is not verified output quality.

Some sources are edited behind-the-scenes films or demonstrations rather than continuous walkthroughs. Keep that distinction during selection. Source and people-use rights remain unverified. The previous Natural download test returned HTTP 403; use accessible original/creator-provided files rather than assuming every source can be downloaded.
